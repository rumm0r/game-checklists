    What can I do now?

      Fill in from my save

      Pick your Octopath Traveler II save file and the checklist ticks what you've already done. The file is read right here in your browser and never uploaded anywhere. Your save itself isn't changed.

      On Steam it's in Documents\My Games\Octopath_Traveler2\Steam\<number>\SaveGames, named like SaveData1.sav (the number is the save slot).

      Backup

      Save every tick and your chapters to a file, or load one back in. Useful before clearing your browser or when moving to another device.

      How this list works
      • Each zone shows its Items to Steal and its Chests. Use the toggles in the sidebar to show either or both.
      • Stealing is daytime only. Only NPCs who are out during the day are listed. Save first: a failed steal lowers your reputation in that town.
      • Chapter tags show which story chapters take you through a zone. Fields carry the chapters of the town they lead to (on the way). Optional zones are side dungeons and places no chapter visits.
      • Prize marks top gear, large Nuts, jams and high-value items. Lv 34 is a zone's danger level.
      • "Knock out the guard" means Ambush, Provoke, Challenge or Soothe whoever blocks the door. At-sea chests and island dungeons need your own ship.
      • Hidden items are sparkles that only appear after you Scrutinize, Inquire, Bribe or Coerce the person named. Ticking one here also ticks it on the Scrutinize tab.
      • Amber tags warn about a catch: After Meylan's quests, a boss to beat first, a quest item, or a spot the guides don't pin down.
      • Set My progress in the sidebar to dim or hide zones your chapters haven't reached yet.

      Reading the tags
      • A tag like Throné Ch 1 means that chapter must be finished before the story appears. A ✓ means you've reached it.
      • Set your chapters under My progress in the sidebar. Stories you can't start yet are then dimmed or hidden, and the rest say Available now.
      • Underlined place names in the steps open that zone. Clue links open the person who gives the information the story needs.
      • Steps say when a person only shows up at night.

      Scrutinize & info

      Townspeople worth questioning: hidden items, side-story clues, better steal odds, new shop stock and more.

      0 / 0
      Which ability to use
      • By day: Osvald's Scrutinize or Castti's Inquire. Both are level checks; a failure lowers your reputation in that town.
      • By night: Hikari's Bribe (costs money, never fails) or Temenos's Coerce (a battle you have to win).
      • Each person shows Day and/or Night depending on when they're out, so you know which pair of abilities works.
      • Important means hidden items, side-story clues, thieving tips (better steal odds for Throné in that town) and new shop stock. Inn discounts and path-action boosts are shown when you turn Important only off.
      • Hidden items appear as a sparkle once you learn about them; walk over and pick them up.
